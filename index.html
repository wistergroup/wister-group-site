Services

What We Deliver

Two distinct service tracks, each led by a dedicated principal — together representing a comprehensive consulting offering for life sciences organizations.

Jean Mastrangelo Clinical Research
Clinical Trial Management & Operations
End-to-end clinical trial support — from study start-up and site selection through monitoring, data management, and close-out — aligned with ICH-GCP and sponsor requirements.
Regulatory Strategy & Submission Support
Guidance on regulatory pathway selection, IND/NDA/BLA strategy, and submission document preparation for FDA and international health authorities.
Protocol & SOP Development
Authoring and reviewing clinical protocols, informed consent documents, and standard operating procedures to meet current regulatory standards.
GCP Compliance & Audit Readiness
Gap assessments, audit preparation, and remediation support for sponsors, CROs, and clinical sites requiring GCP compliance strengthening.
Pharmaceutical Operations Advisory
Strategic guidance for pharma and biotech organizations navigating process optimization, quality system development, and operational scale-up.
Mark Mastrangelo Emergency Response & Safety
CPR & AED Certification Training
Certified CPR, AED, and cardiac emergency response training for organizations — customized to workforce size, environment, and regulatory requirements.
First Aid Program Development & Training
Comprehensive first aid curricula tailored to pharmaceutical and industrial settings, ensuring employees can respond effectively to workplace medical emergencies.
Emergency Response Planning
Design and implementation of site-specific emergency response plans, including evacuation procedures, medical response protocols, and crisis communication frameworks.
OSHA Compliance & Safety Programs
Written safety program development, hazard assessments, recordkeeping systems, and OSHA-aligned training to support regulatory compliance and workforce protection.
Safety Culture & Workforce Education
Building durable safety cultures through instructor-led training, tabletop exercises, and ongoing education that keeps preparedness front and center.
